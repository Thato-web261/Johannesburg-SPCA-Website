How adoption works

  1. Browse the animals and send an adoption enquiry.
  2. We contact you to confirm the animal is still available.
  3. Visit the shelter for a meet-and-greet.
  4. Complete the adoption application.
  5. We carry out a pre-adoption home check.
  6. Pay the adoption fee and deposit.
  7. Take your new family member home.
