Education Programme

Our education programme works with schools to teach children about animal welfare, responsible pet ownership, and kindness towards animals, helping to build a more compassionate community for the future.

We run classroom talks, shelter visits, and holiday workshops for learners, and provide teachers with resources to keep animal welfare part of the conversation year-round.
